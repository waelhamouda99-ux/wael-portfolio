# Wael's Brand Studio

Create a modern, professional one-page portfolio website for Wael Hamouda, a Digital Marketing Specialist & Brand Content Strategist.

COLOR PALETTE (strict 60/30/10 rule):

- 60% Charcoal #171717 (dark backgrounds, dominant tone)

- 30% Yellow Green #A4E500 (accents, headlines, highlights, buttons)

- 10% Deep Purple #4105D3 (small accents, hover states, icon highlights)

- Supporting neutral: Cool White #D7E8FA (text on dark backgrounds)

TYPOGRAPHY:

Primary font: Jacques Pro Bold — used for all headlines, section titles, and the large hero display text. If this font is not available in the build environment, fall back to a bold geometric sans-serif such as Archivo Black or General Sans for the same elements.

Secondary font: A clean modern sans-serif (e.g. Inter or Poppins) for body text, descriptions, and UI elements, since the primary display font is not meant for long paragraphs.

=== SECTION 1: HERO / HEADER ===

- Full-width dark charcoal background

- Large bold headline text "DIGITAL MARKETING SPECIALIST" (or similar strong title) positioned BEHIND a cutout/foreground photo of Wael, similar to this composition style: oversized bold white/lime text spanning the width, with his portrait photo standing in front of it, photo bleeding to the bottom edge of the section

- Small tagline under the headline: something like "Helping brands reach their audience through smart content and strategy"

- CTA button in lime green: "Let's Work Together" (scrolls to contact)

- Subtle navigation bar at top (Home, Services, Tools, Testimonials, Contact) - minimal, transparent over dark bg

=== SECTION 2: ABOUT ===

- Short bio section: Wael Hamouda is a digital marketing specialist helping brands grow their reach through content creation, social media strategy, and paid advertising. He leads a team of photographers and designers to deliver full-scale brand campaigns.

- Two-column layout: text on one side, a secondary photo or abstract graphic accent on the other

=== SECTION 3: SERVICES ===

Grid of service cards (icon + title + short description), dark cards on charcoal bg with lime green icon accents:

1. Content Creation (video & affiches/visuals)

2. Social Media Management

3. Website Creation

4. Logo & Brand Identity (Charte Graphique)

5. Meta Ads Management

6. Photography (via team)

=== SECTION 4: TOOLS I USE (Creative bubble layout) ===

- Section title: "My Toolkit" or "Tools I Work With"

- Instead of a plain grid, display tool logos as floating bubbles of varying sizes, scattered/organic layout (like a bubble chart or floating cloud), softly animated (gentle float/hover motion), each bubble = circular background (charcoal or blue) with the tool's logo centered

- Tools to include: Adobe After Effects, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Canva, CapCut, ChatGPT, Claude, Leonardo AI, Nano Banana, HubSpot

- Use recognizable icons from Simple Icons or Devicon library where available; for AI tools without standard icons (Nano Banana, Leonardo), use a clean generic AI/spark icon with the tool name labeled under the bubble

- On hover, bubble slightly enlarges and shows tool name as tooltip

=== SECTION 5: TRUSTED BY / CLIENTS (placeholder) ===

- Section title: "Trusted By"

- Horizontal row of 5-6 placeholder logo slots (simple grey rounded rectangles with a subtle "Logo" label) — designed so real client logos can be swapped in later easily

=== SECTION 6: TESTIMONIALS (placeholder) ===

- Section title: "What Clients Say"

- 2-3 testimonial cards with placeholder quote text, placeholder name/role, and a 5-star rating icon row — styled as cards on a lime green or charcoal background, easy to replace with real quotes later

=== SECTION 7: CONTACT ===

- Dark charcoal background section

- Bold headline: "Let's Create Something Great"

- Two contact methods only: Email and Phone, displayed as large clickable buttons/icons (mailto: and tel: links)

- Lime green accent icons for email and phone

=== FOOTER ===

- Simple footer with name, copyright, and small social icons (Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp placeholders)

GENERAL STYLE NOTES:

- Smooth scroll, subtle fade/slide-in animations on scroll for each section

- Fully responsive (mobile-first)

- Generous whitespace/padding despite dark theme — should feel premium, not cluttered

- Buttons and interactive elements use lime green as primary CTA color, ocean blue as secondary accent for hover/active states

- Overall mood: confident, modern, creative, slightly bold/edgy — appealing to brands looking for a strong digital marketing partner
